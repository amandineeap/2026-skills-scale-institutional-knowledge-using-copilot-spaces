# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Project Sponsor

### Role Summary
Project Sponsors provide strategic direction and executive backing for the project. They secure funding, remove high-level blockers, and champion the project in leadership circles.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charters and overall scope
- Secure and maintain budget and resource allocations
- Champion the project at the executive and leadership level
- Make final decisions on scope changes and escalations
- Review and approve major milestones and deliverables

### Goals
- Ensure the project delivers measurable business value
- Align the project with organizational strategy and priorities
- Protect the project from shifting priorities or resource cuts

### Typical Communication
- Monthly executive briefings and status updates
- Milestone sign-off meetings
- Ad-hoc escalation calls when critical decisions are needed

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers own the change request process from intake through implementation. They assess the impact of proposed changes, coordinate stakeholder communication, and maintain the integrity of the change log.

### Responsibilities
- Receive, document, and triage incoming change requests
- Conduct impact assessments (scope, schedule, budget, quality)
- Communicate approved and rejected changes to stakeholders
- Maintain the change log and change control register
- Coordinate implementation and rollback planning with the delivery team

### Goals
- Prevent uncontrolled scope creep and unplanned rework
- Ensure all changes are evaluated and approved before implementation
- Keep stakeholders informed of change status and outcomes

### Typical Communication
- Change request intake forms and tracking tickets
- Impact assessment reports and approval records
- Stakeholder notifications via email or project management tools

---

## QA Lead / Test Manager

### Role Summary
QA Leads own the quality strategy for the project. They define test plans, manage test execution, and ensure quality gates are met before releases.

### Responsibilities
- Author and maintain test plans and test strategies
- Coordinate test execution across unit, integration, and acceptance phases
- Track defects and verify fixes meet acceptance criteria
- Define and enforce quality gates and Definition of Done criteria
- Report on test coverage, defect trends, and release readiness

### Goals
- Deliver software that meets acceptance criteria and quality standards
- Reduce post-release defects through rigorous testing
- Provide clear, timely quality status reporting to the team

### Typical Communication
- Test plan documents and test summary reports
- Defect tracking via issue boards
- Sprint reviews and release readiness sign-off meetings

---

## Release Manager

### Role Summary
Release Managers plan, coordinate, and execute releases. They own the release calendar, manage deployment logistics, and communicate release status to stakeholders.

### Responsibilities
- Maintain the release calendar and coordinate release schedules
- Define and enforce release criteria and go/no-go gates
- Coordinate with development, QA, and operations teams for deployments
- Draft and distribute release notes and communications
- Oversee post-release validation and support handoff

### Goals
- Deliver releases on schedule with minimal disruption
- Ensure all release criteria are met before deployment
- Maintain clear, timely communication about release status

### Typical Communication
- Release plan documents and release notes
- Go/no-go meeting facilitation
- Deployment coordination via runbooks and Slack channels

---

## Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Stakeholders are individuals or groups with a vested interest in the project's outcomes. They represent departments, user groups, or business functions affected by the project deliverables.

### Responsibilities
- Provide input on requirements, priorities, and acceptance criteria
- Review and approve key deliverables and milestones
- Represent the needs and interests of their department or user group
- Surface risks, dependencies, or constraints from their area
- Participate in user acceptance testing (UAT) where applicable

### Goals
- Ensure the project outcomes meet their department or user group needs
- Provide timely, actionable feedback to keep the project on track
- Maintain visibility into project status and upcoming changes

### Typical Communication
- Monthly stakeholder update meetings
- Milestone review and sign-off sessions
- Ad-hoc feedback via email or collaboration tools
